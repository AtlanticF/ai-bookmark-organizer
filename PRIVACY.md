Privacy Policy
Effective Date: March 19, 2026

1. Introduction
Welcome to AI Bookmark Organizer. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension handles information when you use our service.

2. Information Collection and Use
AI Bookmark Organizer is designed to help you organize your browser bookmarks using AI technology.

Personal Information: We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Browser Data: To function correctly, the extension requires access to your Bookmarks. This data is processed locally or sent to the configured AI API (e.g., OpenAI) for categorization.

API Keys: If you provide your own API key (e.g., OpenAI API Key), it is stored locally within your browser's secure storage and is never sent to our servers.

3. Data Transmission
The extension only communicates with:

Browser APIs: To read and update your bookmark structure.

AI Service Providers: (User-configured) To process bookmark titles and URLs for organization.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your data to third parties.

4. Permissions
The extension requests the following permissions:

bookmarks: To organize and manage your bookmarks.

storage: To save your settings and preferences.

5. Security
We prioritize the security of your data. Since the extension operates primarily on your local machine and uses direct connections to AI providers, your data remains under your control.

6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page or the GitHub repository.

7. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository: https://github.com/AtlanticF/ai-bookmark-organizer
